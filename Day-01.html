第一步：BBC 6 Minute English 两集（25 分钟）

建议选金融或职场主题，例如 Are you a saver or a spender?、The future of money、Is working from home good for us?。每集 6 分钟，正好适合四遍法。

  1. 第 1 遍 · 盲听。不看文本。只回答一个问题：他们在聊什么？写下 3 个你听清的关键词。
  2. 第 2 遍 · 看文本听。圈出所有"你认识这个词、但刚才没听出来"的地方——这些圈出来的才是你的真实听力盲区，不是生词。

第二步：精听段落 + 影子跟读（20 分钟）

下面这段是我给你写的精听材料，主题正好是你未来的日常工作。用系统朗读（Windows 的"讲述人"、Edge 的朗读功能、或任意 TTS）放出来，语速调到 0.9 倍开始，熟练后回到 1.0 倍。

Cross-border payments rarely travel in a straight line. When a customer in London sends money to a supplier in Singapore, the funds may pass through two or three correspondent banks before they reach the beneficiary. Each bank in that chain applies its own screening checks, and each is legally accountable for the payments it processes. That is why a transfer that looks straightforward to the customer can take several business days to settle — and why compliance teams are involved long before the money moves. Regulators expect banks to know who is ultimately receiving the funds, not simply who placed the instruction.

跟读标记说明

  • rarely 英式读 /ˈreəli/，不是美式的 /ˈrerli/
  • pass through 连读成 /pɑːs θruː/，中间不断
  • correspondent /ˌkɒrəˈspɒndənt/，三个音节弱读
  • legally accountable 重音落在 AC-countable
  • 最后一句是全段重点，读的时候放慢、加重，这是英式正式表达里强调观点的典型做法

跨境支付很少走直线。当伦敦的客户把钱汇给新加坡的供应商时，资金在到达收款人之前，可能要先经过两三家代理行。链条上的每一家银行都要执行自己的筛查程序，而每一家都要为其处理的付款承担法律责任。这就是为什么在客户看来再简单不过的一笔转账也可能要好几个工作日才能结算——也是为什么在资金动起来之前很久，合规团队就已经介入了。监管机构期望银行知道最终是谁在收款，而不只是谁发出了指令。

① 开场
Good morning, and thank you for making the time. My name is [Name], and I'm currently a Compliance Manager at [Bank] in [City], where I've been for the past [six] years.

② 教育与资历
I hold a Master's degree in Law from [University], and I'm a certified anti-money laundering specialist. My background is in financial crime compliance, which I moved into after [three] years in corporate legal practice.

③ 专业领域
My current role covers three areas. First, I'm responsible for the second-line review of transaction monitoring alerts — which means I decide whether an alert should be closed or escalated to our MLRO. Second, I provide advisory support to the business on customer due diligence and sanctions screening queries. Essentially, I'm the person the front office calls when they're not sure whether they can proceed. Third, I own two of our internal policies: the AML policy and the sanctions procedure.

④ 具体案例
One piece of work I'm particularly proud of involved a correspondent banking relationship that had been dormant for several years. When it reactivated, we identified a sudden increase in high-value payments routed through a jurisdiction we'd previously flagged as high risk. I led a lookback review covering eighteen months of activity, worked with the relationship manager to obtain up-to-date ownership information, and ultimately recommended that the relationship be exited. That recommendation was supported at committee level, and it's since been used as a case study in our internal training.

⑤ 为什么是英国
I'm moving to the UK because I want to work within a regulatory framework I've studied closely for years. I've followed the FCA's approach to the Consumer Duty, and the way UK sanctions are implemented through OFSI, and I'd like to contribute to that environment rather than observe it from a distance.

⑥ 收尾
Looking ahead, my goal for the first year is to translate what I already know about financial crime into the specific language and expectations of the UK regime. I'm ready to be useful from day one, and I'm very much looking forward to working with the team.
今天不要背这篇范文。它的作用是让你看到"结构和密度应该长什么样"。你要用同样的骨架，填进你自己的真实经历——用你自己的案例，哪怕说得比范文粗糙，也比背范文有用一百倍。第 6 周你要说的是同一套内容，只是会更流利、更准确。

05WRITE

写作：5 句岗位职责，从口语层升到专业层

今天只写 5 句，但每句都要改三遍

35 分钟

同一个意思，英语里有三个不同的层次。你的目标是把默认输出从 Level 1 直接提到 Level 3——不是靠背单词，而是靠换句式。

示范：把一句话升三次级

Level 1 · 口语化
I look at the alerts and decide if we need to report.
Level 2 · 职场规范
I review transaction monitoring alerts and decide whether they should be escalated.
Level 3 · 合规专业
I am responsible for the initial review and triage of transaction monitoring alerts, and for determining whether escalation to the MLRO is warranted.

变化在哪：look at → review and triage／if we need to → whether ... is warranted／report → escalate to the MLRO。名词化和被动语态是合规写作的两个关键杠杆。

五个万能句式骨架（今天务必记住）

  • I am responsible for + 动名词　→　I am responsible for reviewing...
  • My role involves + 动名词　→　My role involves providing advice on...
  • I provide advice / support on + 名词　→　I provide advice on customer due diligence...
  • I am accountable for ensuring that + 从句　→　I am accountable for ensuring that our policies remain...
  • This entails + 动名词 + in accordance with + 名词　→　This entails... in accordance with regulatory expectations.
1. I am responsible for the second-line review and triage of transaction monitoring alerts.
2. My role involves providing advisory support to the business on customer due diligence and sanctions screening.
3. I own the bank's AML policy and sanctions procedure, and I am accountable for ensuring they remain aligned with regulatory expectations.
4. I lead lookback reviews and internal investigations where financial crime risk is identified.
5. I act as the primary point of contact for the MLRO on escalations, and I liaise with the Financial Intelligence Unit where a report is required.
怎么用：先把你的 5 句中文职责写下来，然后用这五个骨架逐句翻译。不要直接抄上面这五句——你的职责和我不一样，句式一样就够了。写完后把英文读出声，凡是读起来拗口的地方，大概率还是中式英语，回去改。
