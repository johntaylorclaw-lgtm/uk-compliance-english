Maya: What do we have on the underlying payment?

Maya: And the end-user? Any indication of a military end-user?

Priya: That is precisely what the 50% Rule is for. Circumvention works by re-papering the same trade through a gap in the sectoral sanctions.

今天要建立的是合规工作里最常用的一种发言结构：在信息不全时，先说清"现在能确认什么、还缺什么"。这套说法能让你在不放行的时候不显得在推诿，在放行的时候有据可依。用下面的骨架把今天那笔付款说一遍（1 分钟，出声）。

「先确认、后表态」骨架（照着填，然后整段说 3 遍）
· On the facts we have, this is a potential match, not a true match — the fuzzy matching score is eighty-seven per cent.
· What we cannot confirm is the end-use, and that gap is material.
· So the position is: we hold the payment in blocked transaction status and escalate to the MLRO today.
语调规则（今天必须练对的三条）：① 第二句用 "What we cannot confirm is…" 开头时，重音落在 cannot 和 end-use 上，其余全部弱读——这是英式表达的典型"轻-重-轻"节奏；② 列表最后一项 And that is why… 用降调收尾，表示"结论已定"；③ 说 "eighty-seven per cent" 时两个重音都要敲出来，数字在合规场景里说错就是事故。
