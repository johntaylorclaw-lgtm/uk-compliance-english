精听脚本 B：一份举报进来之后的第一天（内部会议）真实语速 · 今天的核心材料 · 30 分钟
这是你入职后最可能第一周就碰上的场景。周五晚上通过 speak-up channel 收到一条受保护的披露（protected disclosure）——有人说某个团队的贷款审批有猫腻。周一上午，三个人坐在一起：Maya（合规官，主持）、Ruth Ellery（金融犯罪部门负责人，批准）、Greg Jarvis（业务负责人，被质询）。
这场会议的核心不是「谁对谁错」，而是顺序和边界：先下 legal hold（谁都不能删东西）→ 再定调查范围 → 再决定谁能知道 → 最后才谈纪律和监管移送。顺序错了，后面全部无效。
用法：用 Edge「朗读此页」播放。第 1 遍 0.9 倍速盲听，第 4 遍回到 1.0 倍速。先自己听、自己猜，猜完再看下面的拆解表——顺序反了就没效果。
Maya: Thanks for coming. We received a protected disclosure through the speak-up channel on Friday evening.
Greg: Through the — is that the hotline? I thought that was for HR matters.
Maya: It's the whistleblowing procedure. Since 1998 it has been the route for reporting financial crime, and the disclosure is protected.
Ruth: Before we discuss the substance, I want three things on the table: a legal hold, an instruction on scope, and an out-of-hours contact route.
Maya: The legal hold went out last night. Mailbox, shared drive, and the two laptops — and the preservation notice is with the IT team.
Greg: Are you telling me nobody can delete anything?
Maya: I'm telling you that if anyone deletes anything, we stop being able to run a fair process. That is the point of it.
Ruth: Also — and I want this minuted — nothing goes to the business beyond Maya and me until the scope is agreed.
Greg: That's going to slow everything down. My people can't run a process without knowing why.
Maya: I understand the pressure. I am not saying no to the business. What I am saying is that a premature disclosure is itself a detriment.
Greg: Fine. How long does an internal investigation take?
Maya: Six weeks for the fact-finding, then a decision. The forensic piece — e-discovery — is what takes longest.
Ruth: And we need outside counsel in from day one, not day thirty.
Maya: Agreed. I've asked for an Upjohn warning to be issued to everyone in scope before the first interview.
Greg: What is it?
Maya: It tells them they must not keep the material, and that talking to us doesn't stop them taking independent legal advice. Without it, the whole exercise may not be privileged.
Ruth: And before we go anywhere near a disciplinary process — remember that summary dismissal is a very blunt instrument here.
Maya: Agreed. Detriment protection comes first, the disciplinary process comes second, and the regulatory referral decision comes last.
Ruth: If it turns out to be a management failure rather than a rogue individual, then this becomes a lessons learned review, not a prosecution.
Greg: Can I say one thing? I'd rather hear this from you than from the regulator.
Maya: That's fair. That's why we do the referral assessment ourselves — and why the file records exactly what we knew, and when.

「程序排序」骨架（照着填，然后整段说 3 遍）
· Here is what we know: a protected disclosure came in through the speak-up channel on Friday evening.
· The first step is preservation — a legal hold went out last night, and the preservation notice is with IT.
· The second step is scope and privilege: independent counsel from day one, and an Upjohn warning before the first interview.
· The third step is the people question — detriment protection first, the disciplinary process second, and the regulatory referral decision last.
语调规则（今天必须练对的三条）：① 每一句开头的 The first step / The second step / The third step 要等距、重读，这是整段话的骨架，节奏一乱，听众就抓不住顺序；② — 破折号处要停半拍再接下一项（preservation 后面、privilege 后面各一次），停顿本身就是"顺序"的信号；③ 最后一句三个名词短语逐级加重（first → second → last），last 用降调收尾，表示"这一步不会提前"。
