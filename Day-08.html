BREACH RESPONSE 骨架（三段 · 照抄结构，内容用自己的话）
① Pin the clock — The notification window is seventy-two hours from awareness, not from confirmation. We are treating the clock as having started at [09:47].

1. Awareness — Clock start: [time]. Basis: [alert / report]. Conservative view adopted because the organisation, not the first individual, was aware.
2. Scope — Data affected: [__]. Special category data involved: yes / no. Approximate number of data subjects: [__]. Systems: [__].
3. Risk assessment — Is it likely to result in a risk to rights (financial loss included)? yes / no. If notifiable, notification to affected people required: yes / no.
4. Lawful basis — Basis relied on for the processing that exposed the data: legitimate interests / consent / contract / legal obligation. Consent is not a substitute here.
5. Cross-border — Any flow outside the UK? yes / no. If yes: transfer risk assessment completed? adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses covered this flow? TRA owner: [name].
6. Regulatory interface — Initial notification to ICO: signed by DPO (independent of the business line). Any law enforcement request route considered separately.
7. Documentation — Records of processing activities updated. What we knew and when, minuted. Decision log kept from this point.
8. Next four hours — [action] — owner — by when] × 3
9. Remediation — [access revoked / password reset / supplier informed]. Customer-facing communication drafted: [yes / deferred with reason].
