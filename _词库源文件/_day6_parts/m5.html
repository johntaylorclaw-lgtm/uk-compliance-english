任务 A：用精讲词写 5 句（15 分钟）

从今天 30 个精讲词里选词，每句至少用 2 个，写成你在真实工作中会说的话。写完朗读一遍，读着拗口的句子必须改到顺为止。今天特别要求：至少 2 句里要用上 disclose / disclosure 这一对（动词与名词），把清浊差异写进句子里感受一次。

示例（可以直接沿用句式）
· The regulatory referral decision comes last, after the internal investigation and the lessons learned review.
· Without an Upjohn warning, the internal report may not be privileged, and we may have to disclose it.
· A consent order is not a disclosure — one is imposed by the regulator, the other is made by the firm.

任务 B：写出「内部调查启动备忘」四行骨架（10 分钟）

昨天写的是「装货前审查备忘」，管的是一笔交易；今天这份管的是一件事的启动。英国合规团队在收到举报的当天就要出这样一份东西，四行结构固定，改的只是内容——而且它的顺序本身就是要点。

INVESTIGATION OPENING NOTE（骨架，照抄格式，内容用自己的话）
Trigger: [Protected disclosure received via the speak-up channel on Friday evening]; allegation concerns [__]. No insider threat indicator at this stage; no dawn raid risk assessment required.
Preservation: Legal hold issued [last night] — mailbox, shared drive, [two] laptops. Preservation notice served on IT. E-discovery collection to be scoped by external counsel; no deletion is permitted from this point.
Privilege: Independent counsel instructed from day one. Upjohn warning to be issued to everyone in scope before the first interview. Advice privilege and litigation privilege to be assessed separately for each workstream; assume no privilege until confirmed.
People & next steps: Detriment protection confirmed to the reporter in writing; no contact with the business beyond the investigation team. Disciplinary process is not a first step. Regulatory referral assessment at the end of fact-finding, with the file recording what we knew and when.
为什么今天这份比昨天那份更难：昨天四行里的内容是已知的（船、货、名单命中），今天第一行 Trigger 里全是还不确定的东西。所以英式写法的关键不是把话说满，而是把"还没查的"和"已经做的"分开写——assume no privilege until confirmed 和 no deletion is permitted from this point 这两句，是整份备忘的骨头。

任务 C：回炉昨天的作业（5 分钟）

打开 Day05_装货前审查备忘_骨架.md，检查三件事：

  1. 重音——把 ownership chain / catch-all control / sanctions nexus / economic crime / voluntary self-disclosure 五个词组圈出来，手指敲桌子打重音，每个读三遍。今天新增一条硬规则：名词与动词靠重音区分（EXport / exPORT）。
  2. 英式 vs 美式——把 -ise / -isation / -our / -re / -ce 的词全部检查一遍：organisation / optimisation / behaviour / centre / licence / offence / proceeds。offence 的 -ce 今天在词表里正式出现。
  3. 结构复用——对比今天的四行和昨天那份，发现两处可复用的句式（比如都是"先说什么、后做什么"的收尾方式），把它们抄到错题本最后一页，作为你自己的模板库。
