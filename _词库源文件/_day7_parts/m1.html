B
精听脚本 B：监管检查开场会（内部会议）真实语速 · 今天的核心材料 · 30 分钟
这是你入职后第一周最可能碰上的第二个场景。周二上午，FCA 的检查组（Supervisor Priya Nair） arrive 伦敦办公室，要求看某三项业务的记录。对方三人：Alan Whitfield（业务负责人，被质询）、Maya（合规官，主持）、Ruth Ellery（金融犯罪部门负责人，今天不在场——但她的名字出现了）。
这场会议的核心是范围与边界：检查组要什么、我们能给什么、什么必须先确认合法基础才能给。而且它把今天词表里的整个监管体系串起来了——FCA / SYSC / COBS / threshold conditions / fit and proper / final response letter。用法：用 Edge「朗读此页」播放。第 1 遍 0.9 倍速盲听，第 4 遍回到 1.0 倍速。先自己听、自己猜，猜完再看拆解表。
Priya: Thank you for making time. This is a routine examination under our supervisory powers — I want to be clear about that at the outset.
Maya: Understood. Would you prefer to start with scope, or with the specific threshold conditions questions?
Priya: Scope first. And I should say up front — this is not an enforcement visit. If we find something, we will tell you before we write anything.
Alan: That's helpful to hear. We did wonder — after the last review, are we still subject to the remediation plan?
Priya: Partly. Two of the three items are closed. The third — customer money — stays open, and that is why I am here.
Maya: Then let me take you to where that sits. Client money rules are in COBS, and the governance requirements sit in SYSC.
Priya: Right. And under SYSC, who is accountable for that sign-off?
Maya: The senior manager function. Each senior manager has a set of prescribed responsibilities, and client money sits with one of them by name.
Alan: That was us a few years ago. Ruth's function, before the regime changed.
Priya: The regime is not the point. Fit and proper is the point. I don't care who holds the function; I care whether the person holding it could explain the control to me.
Maya: That's fair, and it's the right way to ask. Can I ask what you're actually looking for today?
Priya: Three things. The safeguarding procedure. The last two years of internal reconciliation records. And one notifiable matter from 2024.
Alan: The notifiable matter — is that the one that was disclosed to you last spring?
Priya: It was disclosure at the time, yes. Whether it met the threshold is a separate question, and I'd rather we looked at that together than in a letter.
Maya: We'd prefer that. Our position is that inside information was disclosed to the market at the same time, so there was no unlawful disclosure.
Priya: That argument turns on inside information and on timing. I'd like the wall-crossing records, and then the internal information barrier you operated at the time.
Maya: We can produce the wall-crossing log today. The Chinese wall policy is in the compliance manual — chapter four.
Alan: If we supply everything today, when do we get the final response letter?
Priya: There isn't one on this visit. A final response letter follows a decision, and there is no decision until the file is complete. If you wanted redress for the delay, that is a separate process — your complaint would go to the Ombudsman, not to me.
Maya: Understood. Then let us set out what we produce today and what we owe you by when.
Priya: One more thing, and this is the part I would remember if I were you. Nobody at your firm should be cease and desist order-ed into a panic by this visit. Tell your people it is routine, tell them what we asked for, and tell them what you will do.
Maya: That's exactly how we'll brief it. Thank you for being upfront with us, Priya — genuinely it makes this easier.

词块听写：今天的词在真实句子里长什么样把"认得"变成"写得出" · 25 分钟
今天听写的两个新难点：① 缩写的逐字母读法（FCA / SYSC / COBS / PRIN / SM&CR / SCA / FOS）——这是第 1 周从未考过的新题型，今天一次考七个；② 专有名词的大小写与词形（Chinese wall / Wall's Road / BoE）——听写时大写容易写错。
