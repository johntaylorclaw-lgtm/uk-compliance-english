B
精听脚本 B：数据泄露被发现后的头两个小时（内部电话会）真实语速 · 今天的核心材料 · 30 分钟
这是你入职后最可能被半夜叫醒的场景（夸张版）。周二上午 9:47，系统监控弹出一条告警；10:02 安全团队确认是真实泄露——一个外包运维账号在非工作时间登录，批量导出了客户资料库。四人在线：Priya（数据保护官 DPO，主持）、Alan（IT 安全，负责技术）、Maya（合规，负责监管接口）、Ruth（业务，负责客户影响）。
这场会的核心不是「怎么被入侵的」，而是三件事：① 时钟从哪一刻起算（GDPR 说「aware of it」，但 aware 何时算「知悉」，这正是今天争议的焦点）；② 这 72 小时里能对外说什么（多数团队第一反应是「先别告诉客户」，这可能是错的）；③ 谁签字（DPO 独立于业务，这一点是 GDPR 的核心设计）。用法：用 Edge「朗读此页」播放。第 1 遍 0.9 倍速盲听，第 4 遍回到 1.0 倍速。先自己听、自己猜。
Priya: Right. Before we go round the table — what time does the clock start? I want it minuted.
Alan: Depends what you mean by aware. My team became aware at nine forty-seven, when the alert fired.
Priya: That's not the right question. The right question is when the organisation became aware — not when the first person did.
Maya: Which means we may already have started the clock before we confirmed anything.
Priya: Exactly. And we cannot move it back. So let us be conservative: we are treating the clock as having started at 09:47, and we will note the competing view.
Ruth: So what's our position with affected customers today? Some of them will notice the data was accessed.
Priya: If the breach is likely to result in a risk to their rights — financial loss included — then we notify them. Not as a favour. As an obligation.
Ruth: Will we get the volume of complaints that comes with telling four thousand people?
Priya: We will. And we would get a worse one by not telling them and it coming out later. I am not saying tell everyone today. I am saying the decision is not ours to postpone.
Alan: On timing — the notification window is seventy-two hours from awareness, not from confirmation.
Priya: And if we cannot identify the affected people within the window, we notify the regulator anyway and explain why.
Maya: Then I need the lawful basis question answered before we draft anything. Are we relying on consent, or on legitimate interests?
Priya: Legitimate interests, for the fraud monitoring that caught this. And no — consent is not a substitute. You cannot consent your way out of this.
Ruth: Who signs the initial notification to the ICO?
Priya: I do. That is the point of having a DPO — the data controller retains responsibility, but the DPO is the one who signs, and I do not report to the business.
Ruth: That is worth saying out loud to my team, actually. Because they will ask me, and I want to give them the right answer.
Alan: One more from me. The export was from the client database. If any of it went outside the UK, we have a second problem — cross-border transfer on top of everything else.
Priya: Then we do a transfer risk assessment today, and we check whether our adequacy decision or our standard contractual clauses actually covered this flow. That is a separate workstream, not a footnote.
Maya: Can I set out the next four hours before we get pulled into something else?
Priya: Please do. And put the deadline at the top of the page — seventy-two hours from 09:47, not from whenever this call ends.

词块听写：今天的词在真实句子里长什么样把"认得"变成"写得出" · 25 分钟
今天听写的两个新难点：① -tion 词的重音落在词尾（notification / categorisation / rectification / adequate）——听写时容易写成自己熟悉的那个词；② 数字与时间（seventy-two hours / 09:47 / nine forty-seven）——听写时数字记不下来，整句就废了。
做法：用 Edge 朗读下面的 16 句（慢速档 0.8 倍），一句一停，写下来。写完再看答案。拼写错和听不出一样算错。

盲区预测清单（20 条 · 数据保护词专用）

下面这些词，你十有八九会在接下来的几天听力里听不出来。命中一条记一条进错题本。第 ③ 组（-tion 重音）今天最该重点自查。

#词你的默认读法（易错）实际读音原因
1notification/ˌnəʊtɪfɪˈkeɪʃən//ˌnəʊtɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/词尾 /ʃn/ 压成一个音节，不加 /ən/；重音在 -TI-。今天第一高频词
2categorisation重音在 -GOR-/ˌkætəɡəraɪˈzeɪʃn/重音在最后音节 -TION；英式拼 -isation，元音 /aɪ/
3rectification重音在 -TI-/ˌrektɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/重音在 -CA-（不是 -TI-）；动词 rectify 却在首音节
4subject (名)重音在后/ˈsʌbdʒɪkt/「数据主体」是名词，读首重音。subject to 才读后重音
5subject to重音在首/səbˈdʒekt/「受制于」是短语，读后重音，词尾元音变 /e/。今天第① 号陷阱
6erasure / erase都读成 /z//ʒə/ · /z/名词浊音、动词清音。right to erasure 是名词
7disclosure / disclose都读成 /z//ʒə/ · /z/同一机制，第三次出现。三次还错必须专项攻坚
8costs/kɑːsts//kɒsts//ɒ/ 是英式专属短圆唇音，不卷舌。costs and charges disclosure
9personal/ˈpɜːsənəl//ˈpɜːsənl/词尾 /ənl/；/ɜː/ 不卷舌。personal data 是今天的基石词
10controller/ˈkɒntrələ//kənˈtrəʊlə/重音在第二音节 -TROL-；词尾 /ə/ 不加 /r/
11processor/ˈprəʊsesə//ˈprəʊsesə/词尾 -er 读 /ə/ 不加 /r/。与 controller 的区别靠重音位置
12DPO读成一个词「迪波」/ˌdiː piː ˈəʊ/逐字母读三个音。这是签署初始通报的人
13ICO读成「eye-co」/ˌaɪ siː ˈəʊ/逐字母读三个音。英国信息专员办公室
14consent重音在首音节/kənˈsent/重音在第二音节 -SENT-。GDPR 六种合法性基础之一
15legitimate interests读成五音节均重/lɪˈdʒɪtɪmət/重音在第二音节 -TI-，第三音节弱读 /ɪ/。最常用的合法性基础
16transfer (名)一律读动词重音/ˈtrænsfɜː/名词首重音、动词后重音。cross-border transfer 是名词
17adequacy结尾读 /əti//ˈædɪkwəsi/结尾 /əsi/ 不是 /əti/；adequate 结尾是 /ət/
18clauses读成单音节 /klɔːz//ˈklɔːziz/两音节 + 结尾浊音 /ɪz/。Standard Contractual Clauses 的结尾
19prohibition重音在首音节/ˌprəˌhɪbɪˈʃn/重音在第三音节 -HI-；pro- 前缀弱读 /prə/
20foreseeable重音在首音节/fɔːrɪˈsiːəbl/重音在第三音节 -SEE-，共四个音节。Consumer Duty 的法定表述
一、本段出现频率最高的连读块（整块记住，不要拆开听）
· data controller → /ˈdeɪtə kənˌtrəʊlə/　· data breach → /ˈdeɪtə bretʃ/
· notification window → /ˌnəʊtɪfɪˈkeɪʃn ˈwɪndəʊ/　· subject access request → /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt ˈækses rɪˈkwest/
· lawful basis → /ˈlɔːfəl ˈbeɪsɪs/　· transfer risk assessment → /ˈtrænsfɜː rɪsk əˈsesmənt/
· standard contractual clauses → /ˌstændəd kənˈtræktjuəl ˈklɔːziz/（三个首重音，打三拍）
· cross-border transfer → /ˌkrɒsˈbɔːdə ˈtrænsfɜː/（两个 /ɔː/ 都不卷舌）

二、今天新学到的弱读（功能词继续降级）
· we are treating the clock → /wiː ə ˈtriːtɪŋ ðə klɒk/（are 弱读 /ə/，the 弱读 /ðə/）
· if we cannot identify → /ɪf wiː kəˈnɒt aɪˈdentɪfaɪ/（cannot 重音在后）
· notify the regulator → /ˈnəʊtɪfaɪ ðə ˈreɡjəleɪtə/（the 弱读 /ðə/，regulator 词尾 /ə/）
· you cannot consent your way out → /juː kənˈnɒt kənˈsent jə weɪ aʊt/（consent your 连读）
· on top of everything else → /ɒn tɒp əv ˈevriθɪŋ els/（三个功能词连续弱读）
· before we get pulled into something → /bɪˌfɔː wiː ɡet pʊld ˈɪntə ˈsʌmθɪŋ/
· Not as a favour. As an obligation. → 两个短句各自降调落住，中间必须有停顿

三、今天新增的英式 vs 美式 5 条
1. /ɒ/ 是英式专属：costs /kɒsts/、costs and charges、cross-border /ˌkrɒs-/、personal data /ˈpɜːsənl/附近的 policy —— 美式读 /ɑː/
2. 词尾 r 一律不卷：controller /kənˈtrəʊlə/、processor /ˈprəʊsesə/、transfer /ˈtrænsfɜː/、regulator /ˈreɡjəleɪtə/、lawful /ˈlɔːfəl/ —— 美式这五个全卷舌
3. -tion 词尾不加 /ən/：notification /ˌnəʊtɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/、categorisation /ˌkætəɡəraɪˈzeɪʃn/、information /ˌɪnfəˈmeɪʃn/ —— 读成 /ˈkeɪʃən/ 就毁了
4. -isation 是英式拼写：categorisation、minimisation ——美式写 -ization，读音相同
5. -our 名词读 /ə/：favour /ˈfeɪvə/、behaviour /bɪˈheɪvjə/ —— 美式读 /ˈfeɪvɚ/

四、数字与时间（今天最实用的一节）
· seventy-two hours → /ˈsevnti tuː ˈaʊəz/（hours 的 h 不发音；不是 seven-two）
· 09:47 → /ˌəʊ naɪn ˈfɔːti sɪvən/ 或 /ˌzɪərəʊ naɪn ˈfɔːti sɪvən/——逐位读，不读「零点九分四十七」
· nine forty-seven → /naɪn ˈfɔːti sɪvən/ —— 英式 forty 没有 /r/，不是 forty 的美式 /ˈfɔːrti/
· four thousand people → /ˌfɔː ˈθaʊzənd ˈpiːpl/ —— thousand 的 th 读 /θ/，people 读 /ˈpiːpl/
· one month → /wʌn mʌnθ/ —— SAR 的法定期限
