任务 A：用精讲词写 5 句（10 分钟）

从今天 30 个精讲词里选词，每句至少用 2 个，写成你在真实工作中会说的话。写完朗读一遍，读着拗口的句子必须改到顺为止。今天特别要求：至少 2 句里要用上 notification / notification window 这一组，把时间义务写进句子里感受一次。

任务 B：写出「72 小时行动清单」（12 分钟）

这是今天的主交付物。英国合规团队在确认泄露当天就要出这样一份东西，它的核心不是「做什么」而是「什么时候之前做完」——每一行都必须带 deadline 和责任人。写的时候把截止时间写在最上方（今天 B 段最后一句就是这个要求）。

BREACH RESPONSE PLAN（骨架 · 照抄格式，内容用自己的话）
DEADLINE: seventy-two hours from [09:47] — not from when this document is finished.

1. Awareness — Clock start: [time]. Basis: [alert / report]. Conservative view adopted because the organisation, not the first individual, was aware.
2. Scope — Data affected: [__]. Special category data involved: yes / no. Approximate number of data subjects: [__]. Systems: [__].
3. Risk assessment — Is it likely to result in a risk to rights (financial loss included)? yes / no. If notifiable, notification to affected people required: yes / no.
4. Lawful basis — Basis relied on for the processing that exposed the data: legitimate interests / consent / contract / legal obligation. Consent is not a substitute here.
5. Cross-border — Any flow outside the UK? yes / no. If yes: transfer risk assessment completed? adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses covered this flow? TRA owner: [name].
6. Regulatory interface — Initial notification to ICO: signed by DPO (independent of the business line). Any law enforcement request route considered separately.
7. Documentation — Records of processing activities updated. What we knew and when, minuted. Decision log kept from this point.
8. Next four hours — [action] — owner — by when] × 3
9. Remediation — [access revoked / password reset / supplier informed]. Customer-facing communication drafted: [yes / deferred with reason].
为什么第 5 行（cross-border）必须单列：它是最常被漏掉的一层。B 段里 Alan 提了一句，Priya 立刻把它标成「a separate problem, not a footnote」——因为如果你处理的客户数据里有任何部分出境英国，就额外触发一套跨境传输义务，而那套义务有自己的时间线和自己的评估文件。英国合规团队最常见的失误就是把这一行省掉。

任务 C：数据保护自查三问（8 分钟）

用英文回答下面三个问题。这三个问题是你入职第一周就会被问到的（入职面谈、年度培训、监管检查开场都会用），所以要写成能直接说出口的口语。

  1. 如果一个客户问「你们拿我的数据做什么」，你用哪两个词回答责任归属？为什么不是两个都答？
  2. 如果同事说「先别告诉客户，等查清楚再说」，你用哪三句话回应？
  3. 如果你的处理依赖 legitimate interests，你在文件里必须留下什么？
示例（第 1 问）
· I would say we are the data controller and the cloud provider is our processor. That is not splitting hairs: the controller decides why and how the data is processed, and it is the controller that carries the notification obligation.
示例（第 2 问）
· I understand the pressure. What I am saying is that the notification window runs from awareness, not from confirmation. If we cannot identify everyone in time, we notify the regulator anyway and explain why.
第 2 问的答案里必须包含「not from confirmation」这个区分——这是今天整段脚本的核心争点，也是你未来几年最常被追问的一句话。如果你的回答里没有「aware 是组织层面的知悉，不是第一个人的知悉」这个意思，你的回答是不完整的。
